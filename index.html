ColorSumi

A calm colour-by-number app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Pick a picture, tap a colour, and paint it in with your finger or Apple Pencil. No tracking, and the iOS ads can be removed with a one-time purchase. Signing in is optional and only used to carry your progress between devices.
